Water ponding vs waterproofing test: what is the difference?

Compare the inspection scopes before arranging your property inspection in Kuala Lumpur or Selangor.

A water ponding test checks water flow and drainage performance. It is not a waterproofing test. At Zero Inspection, waterproofing testing is a separate scope included in the Comprehensive package.

Common questions

Does water flowing to the drain prove the area is waterproof?

No. Drainage flow and waterproofing are different inspection scopes. A drainage-flow check should not be described as proof of waterproofing.

Which should I choose for my property?

For most condo units, start with Water Ponding Inspection. Comprehensive Inspection is recommended for landed properties and includes waterproofing testing. Share your property type and access arrangements so we can confirm the suitable scope.

How do I prepare for the Comprehensive package?

Arrange key handover for 2 days and coordinate access to the unit below for inspection on the same day. Confirm these arrangements with us before booking.
